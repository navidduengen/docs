<Note>**Sample content:** Expense rules, limits and approval authority must be confirmed by Finance.</Note>

## Before spending

- Confirm the expense has a legitimate business purpose.
- Check whether pre-approval is required.
- Use an approved purchasing channel if available.

## Submit a reimbursement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Collect the receipt">Keep proof of payment and an itemized record where required.</Step>
  <Step title="Add context">Describe the business purpose, date, amount and currency.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose the cost center">Select the relevant team or project if your system requires it.</Step>
  <Step title="Submit for review">Track status and answer any questions from Finance.</Step>
</Steps>

| Field | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Description | Train ticket for project meeting |
| Purpose | Customer workshop |
| Receipt | Redacted sample receipt |
| Approval | Pending finance review |
