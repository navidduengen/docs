title: "Time off and absences"
description: "An example guide to leave requests and unexpected absences."
---

<Note>**Sample content only:** Entitlements, deadlines and approvals depend on local law, employment terms and actual company policy.</Note>

## Planned time off

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check availability">Coordinate proposed dates with your manager and any relevant project team.</Step>
  <Step title="Submit a request">Use the approved HR or time-off system.</Step>
  <Step title="Wait for confirmation">Do not assume a request is approved until its status is confirmed.</Step>
  <Step title="Prepare a handover">Document coverage for outstanding responsibilities.</Step>
</Steps>

## Unexpected absence

Use the company's official absence notification process as soon as reasonably possible. Follow the applicable rules for documentation and return-to-work reporting.

<Warning>This example deliberately does not specify legal deadlines or medical documentation requirements.</Warning>
