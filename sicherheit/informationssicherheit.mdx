## Everyday security habits

1. Enable MFA wherever required.
2. Keep passwords and recovery codes private.
3. Access only information needed for your work.
4. Lock your screen and install security updates.
5. Report suspicious activity promptly.

## Working with sensitive information

Sensitive information may include personal data, customer records, research documents, commercial agreements and secrets.

<Warning>Never put real patient data, research participant data, passwords or access tokens into this demo repository. Before publishing genuine internal material, confirm that the site is access-controlled.</Warning>

## A suspicious message arrives

<Steps>
  <Step title="Stop">Do not open unexpected attachments or enter credentials through suspicious links.</Step>
  <Step title="Report">Follow the company's confirmed security reporting channel.</Step>
  <Step title="Preserve evidence">Follow the security team's guidance rather than deleting potential evidence.</Step>
</Steps>

See [Data classification](/security/data-classification) for sample handling guidance.
