<Note>**Sample terminology.** Replace these entries with the words your organization actually uses.</Note>

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| DRI | Directly responsible individual; the person accountable for an outcome |
| SOP | Standard operating procedure; a documented sequence of work |
| SLA | Service-level agreement; agreed service expectations |
| MFA | Multi-factor authentication; additional identity verification |
| Incident | An unplanned event that disrupts a service or puts information at risk |
| Change request | A proposal to modify a system, process or service |
| Knowledge owner | Person or team responsible for keeping an article accurate |

<Tip>Use consistent terminology in tickets, project pages and documentation.</Tip>
