## Choose the right channel

| Channel | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Team chat | Short questions and quick coordination |
| Email | Formal communication and external correspondence |
| Task tracker | Owners, deadlines and accountable work |
| Knowledge base | Reusable instructions and durable decisions |

## Better meetings

- Share a purpose and short agenda.
- Capture decisions and open questions.
- Assign a named owner to each action.
- Review follow-ups asynchronously whenever practical.

## Write useful documentation

A helpful article answers: **What is this? Who is it for? What should I do? Who maintains it?**

<Check>Assume a new colleague will read your instructions without any previous context.</Check>
