title: "Requests and approvals"
description: "A repeatable example process for internal requests."
---

<Note>**Sample content:** These steps are illustrative and do not define actual approval authority.</Note>

A consistent request process improves traceability and reduces back-and-forth.

## Standard workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit">State what is needed, why, by when and for which team.</Step>
  <Step title="Route">Identify the responsible service or application owner.</Step>
  <Step title="Review">Confirm budget, data protection and security requirements where relevant.</Step>
  <Step title="Approve">Record the decision before access changes or purchases take place.</Step>
  <Step title="Complete">Document the outcome and close the request.</Step>
</Steps>

## Example: requesting software

| Question | Information to provide |
| --- | --- |
| Business need | What problem will the tool solve? |
| Existing alternatives | Can an approved tool meet the need? |
| Data involved | What information will the tool process? |
| Cost | License type and expected users |
| Approval | Appropriate business and technical review |

## Example statuses

- **New:** Received, not yet triaged.
- **In review:** Requirements and approvals being checked.
- **Approved:** Authorized to proceed.
- **In progress:** Implementation underway.
- **Closed:** Outcome documented.
