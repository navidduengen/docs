## Example classification levels

| Level | Sample description | Illustrative handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public | Approved for broad publication | Publish only after authorization |
| Internal | Routine non-public business information | Share with authorized colleagues |
| Confidential | Sensitive customer or business information | Restrict access and use approved storage |
| Restricted | Highly sensitive personal or regulated information | Follow explicitly approved controls and specialist guidance |

## Before sharing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify the data">Determine what the document contains and whether special protections apply.</Step>
  <Step title="Check the audience">Confirm recipients genuinely need the information.</Step>
  <Step title="Use approved channels">Avoid public links or personal file-sharing accounts for internal material.</Step>
  <Step title="Review retention">Follow the applicable deletion and archival policy.</Step>
</Steps>

If you are uncertain, ask the responsible security or privacy contact before sharing.
