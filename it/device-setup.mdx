## New device checklist

- [ ] Verify the device was issued through an approved channel.
- [ ] Sign in with your assigned work identity.
- [ ] Enable screen lock and supported disk encryption.
- [ ] Complete required operating system updates.
- [ ] Enroll in device management if instructed by IT.
- [ ] Install approved applications from trusted sources.
- [ ] Test your connection to required company services.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I cannot finish enrollment">Capture the exact error and contact the approved IT support channel.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An application is blocked">Do not disable security controls. Request the application through the documented review process.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My device is missing">Immediately follow the device-loss reporting process. Do not wait for your next workday.</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
