## Sign in securely

Use your approved work account and any required multi-factor authentication (MFA) method.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the approved sign-in page">Use a trusted bookmark or the application URL provided by IT.</Step>
  <Step title="Verify your identity">Complete the second authentication factor.</Step>
  <Step title="Check your access">Open the applications needed for your role.</Step>
</Steps>

## Common requests

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I forgot my password">Use your company's approved password recovery process. Never share your password with a colleague or support agent.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I need access to an application">Explain which application you need, your business reason and any error displayed. The application owner should approve the appropriate level of access.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My laptop isn't working">Describe the device, symptoms, when the issue started and troubleshooting already attempted. Do not attach credentials or confidential documents.</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
