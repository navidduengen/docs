Welcome aboard! Use this guide to get productive during your first week.

## Before day one

- [ ] Confirm your start date, workplace and manager.
- [ ] Receive instructions for collecting your company device.
- [ ] Complete the documents requested by People Operations.

## On your first day

<Steps>
  <Step title="Meet the team">Get to know your manager, team members and first-week priorities.</Step>
  <Step title="Set up your device">Sign in to your company account and enable multi-factor authentication.</Step>
  <Step title="Verify access">Test email, calendar, knowledge base and role-specific applications.</Step>
  <Step title="Plan your onboarding">Schedule introductions and keep a list of open questions.</Step>
</Steps>

## First-week checklist

| Activity | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Team introductions | Understand responsibilities |
| Security orientation | Know how to protect company information |
| Process walkthrough | Know where to request help and approvals |
| Feedback check-in | Resolve blockers early |
